    - Root Cause: `ConsentGoogleMaps` (line 5 & line 167) is synchronously bundled into the page tree without `next/dynamic` lazy loading. It executes heavy map consent logic and script loading during client-side hydration. Additionally, `ContactForm` and high-res image assets (`inhaberPortrait`) load synchronously on the main thread.
- **11 Pages with Medium Response Time**:
  1. `/` (`app/page.tsx`): Synchronous rendering of multiple heavy client components (`PricingTable`, `ProcessSteps`, `TrustBar`, `FAQAccordion`).
